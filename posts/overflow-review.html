Overflow Anime Review: Peak Unhinged Short-Form Chaos | Otakupath

If you thought you had seen every possible variation of chaotic household romance, Overflow arrives to remind you that the genre can always take things a step further. Coming in as a short-form, fast-paced comedy series, it strips away drawn-out build-ups and throws viewers directly into a storm of outrageous misunderstandings, boundary-blurring antics, and unapologetic ecchi chaos that demands you completely turn your brain off and enjoy the ride.

The setup relies on a classic dynamic: two childhood friends end up staying in the same apartment, leading to a relentless sequence of coincidences, awkward bath encounters, and hilarious over-reactions. What makes it stand out among short-form series is its sheer pacing—there is zero filler, and every single episode delivers a rapid-fire sequence of unhinged comedy that keeps you laughing at how completely absurd the situation is.

Just like other notorious cult classics in the genre, this is an absolute "open mind required" trial by fire. It knows exactly what kind of show it wants to be and never wastes time trying to pretend otherwise. It leans entirely into its scandalous setup with maximum energy, bold comedic timing, and boundary-pushing scenarios that leave you wondering how any of it was approved for production.

If you go in expecting serious character arcs, you will be sorely disappointed, but if you enter expecting a hilarious, wildly inappropriate, and completely unfiltered slice of late-night disaster entertainment, it delivers a short-and-sweet experience that is impossible to forget.
