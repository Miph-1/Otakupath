Kiss x Sis Anime Review: Unapologetic Late-2000s Ecchi | Otakupath

If you're ready to dive headfirst into the absolute pinnacle of late-2000s ecchi trash, Kiss x Sis is an unhinged, shameless classic that demands you leave your judgment at the door. Behind its absurd premise—a middle schooler constantly fending off the aggressive, boundary-pushing advances of his two stepsisters with their parents' full blessing—lies a hilarious, unapologetic comedy that knows exactly what it is and owns every second of it.

Unlike modern harem shows that dance around romance with subtle subtext, this series throws subtlety out the window from episode one. Whether it's ridiculous study sessions, absurd daily antics, or over-the-top temptation sequences, the show leans entirely into its comedy. It’s an absolute trial by fire of outrageously unthinkable situations and boundary-testing antics that will leave you laughing at the sheer audacity of the writing.

Despite the outrageous scenarios, there is an underlying sweetness to how the characters interact in their chaotic household. The OVA episodes in particular showcase some of the most memorable and unhinged moments of the entire run, while the main TV series keeps a slightly tighter focus on the overarching plot.

If you go in expecting pure, ridiculous fun, you'll find a strangely charming, surprisingly sweet slice-of-life disaster that’s impossible to look away from. For those curious about the original manga's 16-year run, you can find details on Anime News Network.
